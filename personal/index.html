Personal insurance

Insurance for you and your household: your home and things, your car, your health and income, and trips.

Your home and things

  • Homeowners insurance HO-3, HO-5, HO-2, HO-8The house you own, your belongings, and your liability.
  • Renters insurance HO-4Your belongings and liability when you rent.
  • Condo insurance HO-6The inside of your unit, your things and your liability.
  • Landlord insurance DP-1, DP-2, DP-3A home you rent out: the building and lost rent.
  • Flood insurance NFIP, private floodRising water, which home policies exclude.
  • Earthquake insurance Policy or add-onShaking damage, which home policies exclude.

Your car

  • Car insurance Personal autoHarm you cause while driving, and damage to your own car.

Extra protection

  • Umbrella insurance Personal umbrellaExtra liability on top of home and car.

Health, life and income

  • Health insurance Employer, Marketplace, Medicare, MedicaidDoctor, hospital and prescription costs.
  • Life insurance Term, whole, universalMoney for your family if you die.
  • Disability insurance Short-term, long-termPart of your income if you can't work.

Travel and pets

  • Travel insurance Trip cancellation, delay, medicalCancelled trips, delays, lost bags and medical care abroad.
  • Pet insurance Accident and illnessVet bills for your dog or cat.

General information, not advice. Policies differ by insurer and by state. What your own policy says is what counts, so use this site to know what to look for and what to ask.
