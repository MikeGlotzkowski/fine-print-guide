Tools, equipment and goods in transit

Despite the name, covers business property that moves: a contractor's tools, a photographer's cameras, materials on a job site, goods on a truck.

Who it's for
Contractors, tradespeople, event businesses, and anyone whose equipment travels.
Is it required?
No.

Covered and not covered

✓ Usually covered

  • Tools and equipment at job sites and in vehicles
  • Materials being installed, until the job is finished
  • Goods in transit
  • Valuable papers and records

✕ Usually not covered

  • Wear and tear, and breakdown
  • Theft from an unlocked, unattended vehicle, in many policies
  • Property that never leaves your premises, which commercial property covers

Real-life examples

Tools are stolen from a locked van overnight.

✓ Usually covered

A contractor's equipment policy usually pays. Check for night-time or visible-entry conditions.

Check your own policy

Find these on your policy or declarations page, or ask your agent:

  • A list of scheduled equipment with values
  • Theft conditions
  • Coverage for rented or borrowed equipment
