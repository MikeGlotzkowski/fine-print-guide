Your professional work · Umbrella, excess

Commercial umbrella

Adds liability protection above your general liability, commercial auto and employer's liability limits, for large claims.

Who it's for
Businesses with contracts that require higher limits, vehicles on the road, or heavy public exposure.
Is it required?
No, but contracts often require $1 million to $5 million in total.

Covered and not covered

✓ Usually covered

  • Claims above the limits of your underlying policies
  • Defense costs

✕ Usually not covered

  • Most things the underlying policies exclude (some umbrellas cover a few, above a self-insured retention)
  • Professional liability, cyber and management claims

Real-life examples

A delivery van crash causes a $2 million judgment; your auto limit is $1 million.

✓ Usually covered

The umbrella pays the second million, up to its limit.

  • Which underlying policies it sits over
