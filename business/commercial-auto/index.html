Vehicles, tools and data · Business auto, HNOA

Commercial auto

Covers vehicles your business owns or uses: liability for crashes and, if chosen, damage to the vehicles. It works like car insurance with business uses allowed.

Who it's for
Businesses with vans, trucks or cars, or whose employees drive for work.
Is it required?
Yes, for business-owned vehicles, at least the state's minimum liability. Trucking and passenger transport have federal requirements.

Covered and not covered

✓ Usually covered

  • Injuries and damage your business vehicles cause
  • Damage to the vehicles, with collision and comprehensive
  • Employees driving business vehicles
  • Rented or personal cars used for work, with hired and non-owned auto

✕ Usually not covered

  • Tools and equipment in the van, which need inland marine
  • Employee injuries, which workers' comp covers
  • Mechanical breakdown

Real-life examples

An employee crashes the company van on the way to a job.

✓ Usually covered

Commercial auto pays the other party's damages and, with collision, the van. The employee's injuries go to workers' comp.

An employee runs a work errand in their own car and causes a crash.

? It depends

The employee's own policy pays first. Hired and non-owned auto protects your business if it's sued too.

Check your own policy

Find these on your policy or declarations page, or ask your agent:

  • Every vehicle and driver listed
  • Hired and non-owned auto included
  • Liability limits your contracts require
