Question

Does my car insurance cover me if I use my car for work?

Short answer

Driving to and from work and the odd errand are usually covered. Deliveries, ride-hailing, carrying tools or goods, or a car owned by your business usually need extra or commercial coverage.

Usually fine on a personal policy

  • Commuting to and from work.
  • Occasional errands like driving to a client meeting or the bank. Tell your insurer you use the car for business; it may change the price slightly.

Usually not covered

  • Deliveries and ride-hailing: personal policies usually exclude driving for pay. Ask for a rideshare or delivery endorsement, or check what the app company's insurance covers and when.
  • Hauling tools, equipment or goods as a regular part of your job.
  • A car titled to your business: it needs a commercial auto policy.

When employees drive their own cars for you

Their personal policy pays first. Your business can still be sued, so many businesses add hired and non-owned auto coverage (hired and non-owned auto), often to a general liability or business owner's policy.
